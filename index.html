✳ A LAB FOR PEOPLE

Where human experience meets possibility.

At the Human-Computer Interaction Lab at the University of Winnipeg, our starting point is people: their everyday experiences, their needs, and the worlds they move through.

We bring a curious, critical lens to the design of interactive technology, connecting questions about what we can build with questions about what we should build.

✳ HOW WE THINK

Start with people.
Stay curious.

Human-centred research makes room for the context around a technology—not just the screen, device, or system itself.

01

Understand context

Listen to people and learn how technology fits into their everyday lives.

02

Make ideas tangible

Sketch, prototype, and co-design to turn open questions into things we can experience and discuss.

03

Reflect, learn, and refine

Study how ideas work in context. Let what we learn guide the next step.
